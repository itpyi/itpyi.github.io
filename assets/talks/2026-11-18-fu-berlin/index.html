General form of every factory: fixed input |0>, Clifford gates and non-Clifford T, CS, CCZ, Pauli faults, measurements, feedforward (possibly through decoders) and postselection; one or many magic states out. Distillation, code switching and cultivation are all of this form. Cost per accepted state follows from the two numbers. Watch the purple tag line on the next slides: the hidden code.

15-to-1 distillation is teleportation through a code

A bent wire is a Bell pair. Apply at one end and project onto : the gate slides around the bend as its transpose (the same gate, since it is diagonal), and the other end carries the magic state .

The same thing as a circuit. A CNOT prepares the Bell pair; the projection becomes an measurement with a known correction on R — teleportation again.

Encode the measured half in the code. on every qubit acts as a logical , so R again receives . The readout gives 4 parity checks: accept only if all pass.

Every is a gate-teleportation gadget. It consumes one noisy magic state, so a faulty input acts as a error on its line.

One faulty input is caught. Its error flips one outcome, and two of the parity checks fire: the run is rejected.

Two faulty inputs are caught too. At least one check always fires for one or two errors.

Three can pass. These three errors fire no check but flip the output. There are 35 such triples, so the output error is about .

Faults do not change the code

A fault, in the coherent 15-to-1 circuit: on code qubit 1 after the encoder. Shown: that qubit, its gadget ancilla and R. Push the fault to the readout, gate by gate.

Nonlinear feedforward

  • Logical-level factories, TQD, escape: after each round a decoder reads the outcomes and chooses the next correction, .
  • A global simplification compiles the whole circuit before any outcome is known — but this circuit depends on the outcomes. Writing the decoder as gates would add many non-Clifford gates.

How can a decision change the circuit without changing what we compiled?

2 · Draw the outcomes

How can we sample round with its physical probability before the later decisions exist?

The problem, before any technique. A dynamical circuit: the gates after round j depend on a nonlinear function of the outcomes so far. Linear feedforward was made coherent on slide 16, but a decoder made coherent would be a large non-Clifford circuit. Two sub-problems, one per slide.

1 · Every decision becomes a Pauli insertion

  • The controlled gate is fixed at compile time. Only depends on the outcomes — exactly like a fault, so the covariance applies.
  • What remains: draw the outcomes that needs with their physical probabilities, before the later decisions are known.
A coherent decoder would add many non-Clifford gates, and a global method simplifies before any outcome is known. Instead the decision enters through a virtual control qubit. f is the user's Python function on reported outcomes only.

2 · Sample the outcomes without the future

The sampling network, every outcome leg open (triangles): and its conjugate as staircases, one step per round. Output and postselection are traced. Nothing is drawn and no decision is known yet.

Summary

I

Extract the genuine non-Clifford degrees of freedom

Inspired by the algebraic structure of codes: the radical of the phase trilinear form recovers the hidden code — deterministic, polynomial time.

II

Compute only the required scalars

Acceptance and fidelity, not the output state — closing the network avoids paying for the output's magic.

1
Pauli covariance for fast sampling. A fault only flips signs: compile once, update per draw.
2
Causality for dynamical circuits. The trace-preserving future lets global reduction handle decoder feedforward.
Two main ideas, then two supporting ones, one per click. I: what is genuinely non-Clifford, found like a code's structure. II: ask only for the two numbers, so the output's magic cancels. 1: Pauli covariance makes sampling a sign update. 2: causality lets a global method sample a dynamical circuit.

Outlook

  • Simulation inside the design loop: codes, schedules, postselection rules and decoders, optimized with the actual gates under realistic noise.
  • A hardness measure that sees how non-Clifford gates are organized, not only how many there are.
  • Beyond Pauli noise: coherent errors as general-angle rotations; tensor-network contraction of the residual coordinates.
